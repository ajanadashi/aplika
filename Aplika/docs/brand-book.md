# Aplika: brand book v1

## Name
**Aplika** comes from "apliko" (Albanian, "to apply") and sounds close to the Macedonian "аплицирај". It is short, easy to say in both languages and says what the product is about: applications.

## Tagline
**English:** Every application, one place.
**Albanian:** Çdo aplikim, në një vend.
**Macedonian:** Секоја апликација, на едно место.

## Colours

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / text | Ink navy | `#0F2A43` |
| Accent / success | Signal teal | `#14B8A6` |
| Highlight / reminders | Amber | `#F59E0B` |
| Background | Soft white | `#F7F9FB` |

Use navy for text and headers, teal for buttons and "done" states, amber only for things that need attention (reminders, due dates). Check contrast: navy text on soft white passes; do not put white text on amber.

## Fonts
- **Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans (bold)
- **Body and UI:** Inter (regular and medium)

Both are free on Google Fonts and support Albanian (ë, ç) and Cyrillic.

## Tone of voice: three words
**Clear. Friendly. Direct.**

- **Sounds like us:** "You applied to Acme 5 days ago. Want to send a quick follow-up?"
- **Does not sound like us:** "We are thrilled to leverage cutting-edge AI to revolutionise your career journey!"
